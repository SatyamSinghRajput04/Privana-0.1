# 🌐 Privana Project Context (.ai/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md)

## 📌 Executive Overview
**Privana** is a private-first, local-first artificial intelligence analytics platform designed to enable business analysts, researchers, and enterprises to explore, query, and visualize sensitive tabular datasets without sending any raw data or calculation payloads to external cloud servers.

---

## 🔑 Core Differentiators

1. **100% In-Browser Execution**:
   - Query execution, schema profiling, statistical computation, and visualization rendering occur entirely within the user's browser sandbox via WebAssembly and Web Workers.
2. **Deterministic Mathematical Reliability**:
   - Large Language Models / AI are restricted to understanding natural language intent and generating a structured `AnalysisPlan`.
   - All numbers, aggregations, correlations, and distributions are calculated by deterministic mathematical engines (never hallucinated by an LLM).
3. **Tabular-Agnostic Generalization**:
   - The platform dynamically adapts to any tabular data structure (CSV, Parquet, JSON) through statistical profiling without pre-programmed domain assumptions.
